A watch face that zooms into a fractal, a little deeper every minute.

The Mandelbrot set is a famous shape from mathematics: zoom in on its edge and it never runs out of detail, with spirals, stars and branching filaments at every depth. Escape Time takes you on that zoom every hour. At :00 you see the whole shape. Every minute you go a little deeper, turning as you go, until at :59 you are 47,000 times closer.

Every hour is a new dive. Your watch picks its own spots to explore, so no two hours, days or watches show the same one. Each view is calculated on the watch itself.

The time is shown in four large digits that are part of the picture, with bands of color flowing out of them.

Easy on the battery: the face holds still and updates once a minute. A flick of the wrist (or a touch on Pebble Time 2 and Round 2) sets the colors flowing for 30 seconds. Battery saver, in the settings, keeps it completely still: one update a minute, nothing more.

Dark mode draws black digits over deep blues, purples and embers, all the time or only at night. Works on every Pebble, from the original to Round 2, in color or black and white. Your settings are kept on the watch, so the face needs the phone only to change them.
