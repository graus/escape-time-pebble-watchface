The time in four large digits, drawn from the Mandelbrot set itself. Every hour, Escape Time dives into the set: at :00 you see all of it, and every minute the view zooms 1.2x deeper toward one of twelve points on its edge (Seahorse Valley, spirals, stars, branching filaments) until at :59 you are 47,000x deep. The next hour dives somewhere new, and the digits always tell you how far down you are. The digits are part of the fractal: white strokes (or black, in dark mode), fine while the dive is shallow and bold where it is deep, with bands of color flowing out of them and swelling in waves. Each minute's view is computed live on your watch, with integer arithmetic precise enough for the deepest minute. To spare your battery the face holds still: each new minute sharpens in, and a flick of the wrist (or a touch on Pebble Time 2 and Round 2) sets the colors flowing for 30 seconds. Every target is a Misiurewicz point, where the set has new detail at every depth, so a dive never runs out of fractal. Works on every Pebble, from the original to Round 2: black-and-white watches show the same face, dithered. Dark mode, in the settings, draws black digits over deep blues, purples and embers, all the time or only for the night hours you choose. The watch keeps the setting: the face needs the phone only to change it.
